# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Leads / Technical Leads

### Role Summary
Engineering Leads set technical direction, guide implementation choices, and align engineering work with delivery priorities. They help the team balance speed, quality, and long-term maintainability.

### Responsibilities
- Own technical design direction and architecture decisions
- Prioritize engineering work and manage technical dependencies
- Coach developers on implementation quality and delivery standards
- Partner with PMs and Product Managers on trade-offs, feasibility, and sequencing
- Identify cross-team risks related to integration, platform constraints, and operational readiness

### Goals
- Deliver robust technical solutions that support project goals
- Reduce delivery friction caused by unclear ownership or architecture drift
- Maintain a sustainable engineering pace with strong standards and technical clarity

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and technical planning sessions
- Dependency tracking with PMs and stakeholders
- Design discussions with developers and QA during implementation and release readiness

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers to translate product priorities into technical execution plans.
- Coordinates with Project Managers to surface dependencies, risks, and delivery constraints.
- Aligns with Product Managers on what is feasible, sequencing trade-offs, and the impact of technical decisions.

---

## Security / Compliance Partners

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Partners ensure project work follows required controls, privacy expectations, and risk-management practices. They help teams make secure decisions early instead of retrofitting controls at the end.

### Responsibilities
- Review project risk areas, security requirements, and compliance obligations
- Define and validate security checkpoints for design, implementation, and release
- Support secure data handling, access control, and incident-preparation requirements
- Partner with PMs and Engineering Leads to assess risk and mitigate blockers
- Escalate critical vulnerabilities or policy concerns with clear remediation paths

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk across project delivery
- Build trust with stakeholders through clear governance and accountability
- Enable safe release decisions with documented controls and evidence

### Typical Communication
- Security review checkpoints and risk assessments
- Policy and compliance guidance during planning and release windows
- Escalation updates during high-risk or incident-driven scenarios

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Advises Product Managers and Project Managers on the implications of requirements, timelines, and launch decisions.
- Reviews technical design decisions with Engineering Leads and Developers before implementation or deployment.
- Coordinates with stakeholders when security controls or compliance requirements change scope or timing.

---

## Data / Insights Leads

### Role Summary
Data and Insights Leads connect delivery work to measurable outcomes. They define the metrics, reporting model, and experiment approach needed to understand whether a project is succeeding.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics, instrumentation plans, and reporting needs
- Monitor adoption, quality, and business impact signals during execution
- Partner with Product Managers on measurement strategy and decision-making
- Support analysis of user behavior, performance, and operational outcomes
- Help translate learnings into backlog prioritization and improvement actions

### Goals
- Improve evidence-based decision making across the project lifecycle
- Connect product and delivery activity to customer and business outcomes
- Create a shared view of progress, impact, and risk through measurement

### Typical Communication
- KPI dashboards, metric reviews, and stakeholder briefings
- Product decision discussions with PM and Product leads
- Cross-functional review of measurable outcomes and release impact

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to validate whether outcomes match the original business goal.
- Reports on operational and customer signals to Project Managers and stakeholders during status updates.
- Helps Developers and Engineering Leads understand the observability and telemetry required for new features.

---

## Release Managers / Enablement Leads

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate deployment readiness, launch activities, and communication around operational readiness. They help keep release activity structured, consistent, and low risk.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release planning, launch timing, and fallback strategies
- Ensure deployment checklists, smoke tests, and rollback plans are complete
- Support communication across stakeholders before, during, and after a release
- Track release readiness across engineering, QA, and support teams
- Align release windows with customer impact, operational constraints, and business priorities

### Goals
- Reduce release risk and operational surprises
- Improve the consistency and clarity of release execution
- Ensure stakeholders are informed and prepared for rollout and support needs

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and launch checklists
- Stakeholder and support notifications before and after deploys
- Post-release assessments and coordination with incident or support teams

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Engineering Leads and Developers to confirm deployment readiness and rollback viability.
- Supports Project Managers in sequencing release activities with broader milestones and stakeholder communication.
- Coordinates with Security and Compliance Partners when approvals or risk reviews are required before launch.

---

## Customer / User Advocates or Design Partners

### Role Summary
Customer and User Advocates ensure the project remains grounded in real user needs, usability, and service quality. They represent the voice of the customer during planning, review, and release decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Gather user feedback, pain points, and needs from research or direct support channels
- Help define user-centered acceptance criteria and prioritization trade-offs
- Validate that solutions are intuitive, accessible, and aligned to user workflows
- Surface issues that may not be obvious from backlog data alone
- Bring customer context into planning, design reviews, and release success criteria

### Goals
- Improve customer value and adoption of delivered solutions
- Reduce usability and experience risk during product development
- Ensure delivery decisions reflect end-user realities, not only internal assumptions

### Typical Communication
- User research summaries and customer insights reviews
- Product and backlog discussions with Product Managers
- Validation checkpoints with design, engineering, and stakeholder teams

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Provides Product Managers with evidence to refine requirements and success criteria.
- Helps Developers and Engineering Leads prioritize what matters most to real users.
- Supports Project Managers by clarifying stakeholder expectations and risks related to customer experience.

- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These additional roles help teams clarify ownership, accountability, and decision-making across a full project lifecycle.
